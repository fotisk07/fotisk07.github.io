I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer at BCG X in Paris.

I studied engineering at CentraleSupélec, specialising in data science and applied mathematics, after two years of classes préparatoires in Paris. I split my final year between CentraleSupélec and ENS Paris-Saclay through the [MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) master's program. Most of my projects are available [on GitHub](https://github.com/fotisk07/MVA-2025).

During my studies, I was president of [Automatants](https://automatants.cs-campus.fr/), the AI student body, founded MUN CS, a Model United Nations club, and participated in the debate club.

I've worked on self- and semi-supervised image segmentation at the [Austrian Institute of Technology](https://www.ait.ac.at/en/), red-teaming multimodal models at Société Générale, and optimising document processing models at BNP Paribas Cardif. I also did consulting work on the AI Act at AIT.

All posts reflect my own opinions, not those of my past, present, or future employers.
